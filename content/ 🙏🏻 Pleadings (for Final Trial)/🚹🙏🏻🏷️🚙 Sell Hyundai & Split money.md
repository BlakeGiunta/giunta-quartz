# Final Trial Notes – Sale of 2012 Hyundai Sonata

## Relief Requested (Final Trial)

Order that the **2012 Hyundai Sonata (VIN 5NPEC4ABXCH411871)** be sold promptly, the **Ally Financial** lien be paid at closing, and **any net proceeds be divided 50/50 immediately** between the parties. .

---

## Why This Relief Is Just and Right

- **Asset preservation:** Motor vehicles depreciate; continued loan payments, insurance, and maintenance drain the community estate.
- **Clarity and finality:** Sale fixes value and ends disputes over possession, payments, and upkeep.
- **Neutral division:** Converts a depreciating asset into cash and divides it evenly; does not require ongoing court supervision.

## Final Decree Language (Decree‑Ready)

### Vehicle Sale and Division of Proceeds – 2012 Hyundai Sonata

1. **Vehicle.** The parties shall sell the motor vehicle described as **2012 Hyundai Sonata, VIN 5NPEC4ABXCH411871** (the “Vehicle”).
2. **Method and Timing.** The Vehicle shall be listed for sale or sold to a dealer (including a dealer buy‑out) within **10 days** of signing of the Final Decree and the sale shall close no later than **45 days** after signing unless extended by written agreement of the parties.
3. **Lien Payoff.** At closing, the lienholder **Ally Financial** shall be paid from sale proceeds to obtain a release of lien and transfer of title.
4. **Net Proceeds Defined.** “Net proceeds” means the gross sale price minus (i) the Ally Financial payoff and (ii) reasonable, customary selling costs necessary to complete the sale.
5. **Immediate Division.** Any net proceeds shall be divided **50% to Petitioner and 50% to Respondent immediately at closing**, by separate checks or wires.
6. **Deficiency.** If sale proceeds are insufficient to satisfy the Ally Financial payoff, any deficiency shall be treated as a community debt and allocated **50/50** between the parties.
7. **Cooperation.** Each party shall sign all documents reasonably necessary to list, sell, transfer title, and complete payoff within **three (3) business days** of request. Failure to cooperate may be enforced by contempt or appointment of a person to sign on behalf of the non‑cooperating party.
