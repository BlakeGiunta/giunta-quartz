1. **Core Summary**

   1. **Background**: The parties’ marital home is in Sachse, Dallas County, at 2612 Herring Circle, where the child had lived and where Father’s nearby family support network is located. The current Bell County case followed an earlier Dallas County divorce filing in late 2023. In that first case, Mother obtained temporary leverage after Father received the divorce papers and hearing notice only shortly before the temporary-orders setting, and Father, without counsel, signed a Rule 11 agreement that became temporary orders placing Mother in the superior temporary position regarding the child and the house.

4. **Argument**

   1. Satisfies classic elements of fraud (w/ Respondent suffering damages).

      _Italian Cowboy Partners, Ltd. v. Prudential Ins. Co._, 341 S.W.3d 323 (Tex. 2011)

      _“The elements of fraud are:_

      1. _a material representation was made;_
         1. She represented the stay in Bell county as temporary, just a few months.

      2. _“the representation was false;”_
         1. The stay could not be just a few months, because she rented out the house we were staying in on a 1 year lease, and rented out our house on a 1 year lease.

      3. _“when the representation was made, the speaker knew it was false or made it recklessly without knowledge of its truth;”_
         1. Ms Giunta signed her name to the contract renting out our house.

      4. _“the speaker made it with the intent that the other party act upon it; and”_
         1. Ms Giunta has admitted telling me that this was going to just be a few months.
            1. E.g. she loved a document articulating it being a few months.
            2. I testify she was saying this.

      5. _the party acted in reliance on the representation and suffered injury.”_
         1. My damages include all the travel and stress involved:
            1. All the driving expenses.
               1. Pickups-dropoffs; court drives; hotel stays etc.

            2. All the access lost to my son.
