# Respondent’s Trial Brief and Proposed Property Division Regarding Community Debts

---

## I. BACKGROUND

1. The parties were married December 2016.
2. During the marriage, the parties incurred various credit card debts and a vehicle loan.
3. Although the credit accounts are solely in Respondent’s name, almost all the debts were incurred during marriage for community purposes.
4. The marital residence located at 2612 Herring is being sold (or has been sold), and the net proceeds are being held in trust pending further order of the Court.

---

## II. LAW

Under **Texas Family Code § 7.001**, division should be "just and right".
**Texas Family Code § 3.003(a)** says, “Property possessed by either spouse during or on dissolution of marriage is presumed to be community property.” This includes debt, and so liabilities incurred during marriage for community benefit are subject to division as part of the community estate. Additionally, under **Texas Family Code § 6.502**, the Court may issue orders necessary for the preservation and protection of property during the pendency of the divorce.
