  - 🙏 Kill "no contact until psych eval" (temp order)

    - The March 26, 2026 order is invalid. Texas Family Code § 105.001(c)(3) provides that, except on a verified pleading or affidavit in accordance with the Texas Rules of Civil Procedure, an order may not be rendered excluding a parent from possession of or access to a child.
      - The closest Texas appellate authority, **In re Sage Barrera**, vacated a materially similar order where no verified pleading or affidavit was filed before the order issued and rejected post hoc cure by later affidavit. (Barrera was powerful because the record affirmatively showed the absence of the required sworn filing until after the order was signed).
